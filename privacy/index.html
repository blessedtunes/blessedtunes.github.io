Privacy Policy

Soul Codes · Last updated 25 September 2026

Your birth details and your readings stay on your phone. Soul Codes has no account and no server that holds your data.

Soul Codes is run by Blessed Samuel in Sydney, Australia ("we"). Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL].

What stays on your phone

Your name, date, time and place of birth, the gender you choose, your computed chart, your reading progress and your settings. The chart is computed on your phone. We never receive any of it. The place of birth comes from a city list inside the app, so the app does not use your location.

Your phone's own backup (for example iCloud) may include this data, under your phone's settings, not ours.

What leaves your phone

  • Purchases. Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) handles payment. We use RevenueCat to check whether Soul Codes Plus is active. RevenueCat receives a random app ID and your purchase record, not your name, email or birth details. We never see your card details.
  • Usage events, if switched on. We may use Mixpanel to count which parts of the app are used (for example "chapter opened"), under a random ID. These events never include your birth details, name or email.
  • Anything you choose to share. A share card or a PDF goes only where you send it.

We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or rent any data.

Notifications

Morning readings, alerts and the trial reminder are scheduled on your phone. No server sends them.

Deleting your data

In Settings, tap Delete my data. Everything is removed from your phone at once. Deleting the app does the same. Your Plus purchase stays with your Apple ID or Google account: use Restore purchase to get it back.

Children

Soul Codes is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and complaints

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters. A future version that adds accounts or online features will say so here before it ships. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, email us first. In Australia you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
